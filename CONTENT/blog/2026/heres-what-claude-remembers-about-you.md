**Work context**

Domi (Máté Domokos) is a Hungarian-speaking author and creator of the MD Megvalósító Rendszer, a 7-volume spiritual/self-realization book series synthesizing contemplative traditions (including Vedic influences, Maharishi, Yogananda) with modern psychology and practical ordering principles — all in Domi’s own voice, without in-text source citations. The series is published under matedomokos.hu and mdmegvalositorendszer.hu, and Domi works in a WordPress environment with responsive HTML/JS/CSS artifacts. Domi’s self-described role is as a channel (“csatorna”) for the REND — not a personal brand or cult of personality.

**Brief history**

*Recent months*

The MD Megvalósító Rendszer has undergone deep philosophical canonization across multiple sessions. Key principles now locked in (by Domi’s explicit decision):

**Series structure:** 7 books. Book 1 = diagnostic/foundational (“gödör”-image: the person is in a pit, ásó still in hand — two choices: keep digging or climb out). Books 2–6 = GPS-like leadership books guiding the journey home. Book 7 = arrival in the REND. Books 1 and 7 are mirror pairs.

**Core philosophy (REND):** The REND (Order/Being) is not an external rule but the source from which humans originate. Lezüllés (decline/drifting) = the REND as inner direction fades. Commitment is foundational but never a burden — freedom is the base. The person can always return to the REND in their own rhythm, even after lapses. The IDŐ-elv: there is more than enough time for everyone to return home; the habit formed is not the lapse but the return. Return is always toward the JELENLÉTBEN ragyogó REND (the REND shining in the present) — never to old habits or substitutes.

**Surrender (TELJES ÁTADÁS / 5th principle):** Even without strength, one aligns fully — giving everything over to the REND, which never lets a person be lost. The REND here is personified as a sustaining reality (“Ő megtart”). This coexists with a non-dual frame (“ÉN VAGYOK = JELENLÉTBEN RAGYOGÓ REND VAN”) — the reader chooses whichever frame serves their liberation. This is an extension of the freedom principle to metaphysical language itself.

**Relational vs. non-dual frame:** Both coexist in the system; neither is imposed. Freedom applies not only to life practice but to metaphysical framing.

**LÁMPA-elv (series-wide):** TISZTA ÖNTUDAT (LÉTALAP/REND, non-invasive) vs. ÖSZTÖNÖS ÖNTUDAT (ego, invasive). The lamp illuminates state, never judges. The ego is not an enemy — it is to be integrated and transcended. The vezető hang (leading voice) is written from tiszta öntudat.

**Hope-frame (terhelés + remény):** Burden is always present in life, but it does not define life — REMÉNY does. Suffering is not a goal, not a merit, not something to eliminate; it is framed: hope gives it meaning. This coexists with the freedom principle.

**MD Küldetés:** “Lelki tehermentesítés” (spiritual unburdening) — reaching many people. Every MD text, chapter, and product must carry this.

**MD 1. Alapelv (canonized):** RENDELTETETT MÉLTÓSÁG HORDOZÓ — capable and called to live/act in alignment with truth, inner order, responsibility, and consistency. Méltóság = MŰKÖDÉS (function), not state/feeling/recognition. Loss = loss of access to strength, not the strength itself. Restoration = bringing existing strength into clean operation. Includes 5 operating rules + 4 daily check questions.

**VISSZAMÉRÉS (canonized):** Not comparison to others — only to one’s own earlier state. Not excuse-seeking or blame of circumstances. Does: establishes the fact of progress/regression, learns from mistakes, continues more attentively. Neither whip nor sedative — fact + next step. Outward comparison forbidden; inward (temporal self-comparison) = the visszamérés itself. Visszamérő (assessment) forms are integral to the system, not appendices; each leadership book has one.

**Consciousness levels:** Used as orientation/navigation tool (like GPS coordinates), not judgment. State, not fault. Only verbs in behavior column (never adjectives). Also applicable to oneself — without self-flagellation. Not attached to one book; accompanies Books 2–6 with deepening maturity (“include and transcend”).

**4th book (15 Hónapos Út):** Structured around the 15-month biological process of infant learning to walk. Mental walking comes first. Stumbling is learning, not error. Walking is always toward something — the outstretched arm (REND). Resolves the relational/non-dual frame tension: for an infant, both are simultaneously true.

**Raw material gathering phase:** Filtering content from matedomokos.hu, ChatGPT conversations, and existing book drafts using a strict criterion: “vezető hang” (calm, present, no longer searching) vs. “kereső hang” (in motion, uncertain). Only the former enters the series.

**Market positioning:** The market assumes users already have “mental walking” ability — MD is the prerequisite: teaching mental walking over 15 months. Not competing with existing systems — preceding them. Maharishi’s triad (healthy body, spiritual health, clear thinking) = the foundation.

**Stylistic rules (canonized):** No in-text source citations (Maharishi, Yogananda, Tolle, Singer, Hill, Roth, Bible — all integrated into Domi’s synthesis). Silent “Köszönet a forrásoknak” page at end of each book. Tone: dogma-free, burden-free. Addressing: mixed — magmondatok use gnomic 3rd person (timeless, aphoristic); body text uses tegező (direct “te”). The switch at magmondatok is natural and marks them as elevated.

**Authorial position:** “Nem az lényeges, ki vagyok — hanem hogy kinek-minek a csatornája vagyok, és hogyan tudtam azzá lenni.” The series speaks through Domi, not about or by him. Protects against personal cult; criticism/praise is not personal.

**Opening of Book 1 / intro-ajánló (canonized):** “Állj meg egy pillanatra. // Egy gödör peremén állsz. Nem fontos, hogyan kerültél ide — az út hosszú volt, és minden lépésnek volt oka. Ami most számít: a kezedben még mindig ott van az ásó. // Két lehetőséged van. Tovább ásol — vagy elindulsz felfelé. // Ez a könyv nem ítélkezni jött. Azért szól, hogy emlékeztessen: a kimászás lehetősége benned van. Mindig is benned volt.”

**Magmondat-archívum (all canonized by Domi):**

1. “Minden, amit teszel, előzze meg a REND-hez való igazodás.”
2. “Ha nem tudja, hogy tudja, nem tudja.” (képesség + tudatosodás; körkörös szerkezet maga mutatja a tartalmát)
3. “Tedd meg, amit itt és most képes vagy megtenni.” (jelenlét-elv + IDŐ-elv gyakorlati párja)
4. “A méltóság nem tökéletesség, hanem következetesség.” (lelki tehermentesítés fő ütőkártyája)
5. “Nem neki van vége, hanem az URALMÁNAK.” (LÁMPA-elv csúcsmondata; ego ≠ LÉTALAP)
6. “A VAN-nak végtelensok arca, formája, ruhája van, a NINCS-nek semmilye nincs…(?)” (filozofiai alapozó, megelőzheti a gödör-képet)
7. MD összegző alapmondat (C-verzió): “Méltóság hordozó vagy. Elkötelezett a legjobb minőségre. Tudod, hogy tudod. És vissza akarsz térni oda, ahol ez működik benned.”

**Állás-járás-futás trió:** “Állás” = not passive pose but ALAP/TALP — the held point from which all action starts. Without standing firmly, there is no starting: only drifting or reacting. Action flows from standing, not after it.

**5th book (MD Rendtartó Szolgáló) — technical build:** 60 chapters + 10 appendices, 85 total pages, burgundy/gold color scheme (Cinzel/Cormorant Garamond/EB Garamond fonts), pageable HTML and JSX React. Source files parsed and cached; final outputs saved. Domi’s firm expectation: cross-check source material against built output before declaring anything complete; each chapter gets its own page.

**Chapter sequence for ALAP könyv writing:** Chapter 5 → Chapter 6 → Chapter 1 (recommended order, established in prior session).
